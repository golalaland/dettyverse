The Creator

Welcome to my world.

I've spent the better part of a decade collecting things — images mostly, sometimes film, occasionally a few hundred words written too late at night to be fully trusted.

DETTYVERSE is what happened when I stopped keeping that collection to myself. It's still, in most of the ways that matter, a private archive. What you see publicly is a fraction of it — the part I've decided is ready to be seen by people I haven't met.

The rest is kept for the people who bother to come inside. Not because it's more scandalous, but because some work is better with an audience that chose to be there.
